How the logistics workflow fits together

1 · Create the shipment

A sender or business records shipment details so the parcel has a traceable place in the logistics workflow.

2 · Receive and record

An office or hub records parcel receipt and keeps the handover associated with the shipment.

3 · Coordinate movement

A transport provider or scheduled movement carries parcels between offices and hubs according to the available route and assignment.

4 · Complete the handover

The receiving office, hub or last-mile agent handles the next step and records the relevant delivery or collection outcome.

Roles in the Kentexa logistics network

Logistics office

The office is an operational point for shipment intake, parcel receipt, dispatch and customer handover.

Super Agent and hub operator

A Super Agent operates a hub where parcels can be received, organised and handed onward within the network.

Agent

An agent supports local collection, last-mile handover or delivery where that service is available.

Transport provider

A transport provider contributes movement capacity between locations and can participate in route and assignment workflows.

What better shipment visibility helps answer

Office teams need to know which parcels have arrived, which are waiting for dispatch, which have been assigned for movement and which require a customer or agent handover. Consistent shipment records and status updates help staff answer those operational questions without relying only on informal messages.

Frequently asked questions

Who uses Kentexa logistics office workflows?

The logistics network is designed for sending and receiving offices, hub operators or Super Agents, last-mile agents and transport providers.

What happens when a parcel reaches a hub?

The receiving team records the parcel at the office or hub and continues the shipment handover workflow so its next movement can be coordinated.

Can transport providers participate?

Transport providers are a defined participant in the Kentexa logistics network. Available routes, schedules and assignments depend on the services enabled.

Does Kentexa support last-mile delivery?

Kentexa's logistics model includes agents for last-mile handover and delivery, subject to local network coverage and enabled workflows.
