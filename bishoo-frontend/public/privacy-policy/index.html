Sera ya Faragha

Ilisasishwa mwisho: Novemba 2026 · Tunaheshimu faragha yako

Sera hii inahusu tovuti ya kentexa.com na programu ya KenteXa kwenye Google Play (com.app.bishoo, iliyoitwa zamani Bishoo Tanzania), inayoendeshwa na Bishoo Intelligence Systems (BiS), Tanzania.

Faragha yako ni muhimu kwetu. KenteXa haiuzi data yako binafsi. Tunakusanya tu kile kinachohitajika kuendesha soko kwa usalama.

1. Taarifa Tunazokusanya

Tunakusanya taarifa zifuatazo unapotumia KenteXa: Taarifa Binafsi: - Jina kamili, anwani ya barua pepe, namba ya simu - Anwani ya utoaji na mahali - Namba ya kitambulisho cha taifa (kwa mawakala na ofisi pekee) Taarifa za Miamala: - Maagizo yaliyowekwa, bidhaa zilizonunuliwa, kiasi cha malipo - Namba za ankara na marejeleo ya miamala - Historia ya ufuatiliaji wa vifurushi Taarifa za Kiufundi: - Aina ya kifaa, kivinjari, anwani ya IP - Kurasa ulizotembelea, muda uliotumika kwenye jukwaa - Maswali ya utafutaji na historia ya kuvinjari kwenye KenteXa Ujumbe wa Inbox: - Ujumbe unaotuma na kupokea kwenye inbox ya KenteXa (pamoja na WhatsApp na Instagram zilizounganishwa na biashara), picha na maelezo ya ndani ya biashara

2. Jinsi Tunavyotumia Taarifa Zako

Tunatumia taarifa zako kufanya: - Kushughulikia maagizo na kuwezesha miamala kati ya wanunuzi na wauzaji - Kutuma misimbo ya uthibitisho wa OTP na arifa za akaunti - Kutengeneza ankara, risiti na namba za kufuatilia - Kukuonyesha machapisho, biashara, bidhaa na huduma zinazokuhusu - Kuzuia udanganyifu na kuhakikisha usalama wa jukwaa - Kuwasiliana na masasisho muhimu ya jukwaa - Kuboresha huduma zetu kupitia uchambuzi wa data Mapendeleo yako: Tunatumia shughuli zako ndani ya KenteXa (unachotafuta, unachotazama, unachopenda au kuhifadhi, mada, jumuiya na biashara unazofuata, tagi na maagizo yako) kuelewa kinachokuvutia na kukuonyesha maudhui yanayokuhusu. Pia tunaangalia jumla ya mahitaji (kwa mfano vitu vingi vinavyotafutwa bila kupatikana) ili kuboresha KenteXa; ripoti hizo ni za jumla ya watu 10 au zaidi na hazikutaji wewe. Wafanyakazi walioruhusiwa wanaweza kuona muhtasari wa mapendeleo ya mtu mmoja kwa ajili ya msaada au usalama tu, na kila mara inarekodiwa. Ujumbe wako wa inbox hautumiki kwa mapendeleo haya. Akili bandia (AI): Tunatumia huduma za AI za watoa huduma wa nje (kama DeepSeek, OpenAI na Anthropic), ambazo zinaweza kuwa nje ya Tanzania, kwa kazi kama kuelewa unachotafuta, kupendekeza maelezo ya bidhaa, kukagua maoni kabla hayajachapishwa (maoni yanayokiuka kanuni yanaweza kukataliwa) na kuchambua mapendeleo. Tunatuma kiasi kidogo kinachohitajika kwa kazi hiyo tu, na hatutumi jina lako wala namba yako ya simu kwa uchambuzi wa mapendeleo. AI haifanyi peke yake maamuzi kuhusu akaunti yako, malipo yako au uthibitisho wako. Ujumbe wa inbox: Wafanyakazi wa KenteXa walioruhusiwa (admin, meneja, huduma kwa wateja na wakaguzi wa maudhui) wanaweza kusoma mazungumzo ya inbox kwa ajili ya usalama, kuzuia udanganyifu, kutatua migogoro na kukusaidia. Kila mazungumzo yanayofunguliwa na mfanyakazi yanarekodiwa pamoja na jina lake. Hatutumii ujumbe wako kwa matangazo wala kuuuza.

3. Kushiriki Taarifa

Tunashiriki taarifa zako tu inapohitajika: Kwa Wauzaji: Anwani yako ya utoaji, jina na namba ya simu vinashirikiwa na wauzaji ili kutimiza agizo lako. Kwa Wasimamizi wa Malipo: Namba yako ya simu inashirikiwa na M-Pesa, Airtel, Tigo au watoa huduma wengine wa malipo ili kushughulikia malipo. Kwa Mawakala wa Utoaji: Anwani yako ya utoaji, jina na simu vinashirikiwa na ofisi na mawakala wa karibu ili kupeleka kifurushi chako. Kwa Mamlaka: Tunaweza kushiriki taarifa na vyombo vya sheria ikiwa sheria za Tanzania zinahitaji hivyo. HATUUZI data yako binafsi kwa wahusika wengine kwa madhumuni ya masoko.

4. Usalama wa Data

Tunalinda data yako kwa kutumia: - Uhifadhi wa hifadhidata uliosimbwa - Muunganisho salama wa HTTPS kwa uhamishaji wote wa data - Uthibitishaji wa JWT token - Usimbaji wa nywila (bcrypt) — hatuhifadhi nywila wazi kamwe - Ukaguzi wa mara kwa mara wa usalama Hakuna mfumo ulio salama 100%. Iwapo kutatokea uvunjaji wa data, tutawaarifu watumiaji walioathirika ndani ya masaa 72.

5. Vidakuzi na Ufuatiliaji

KenteXa inatumia: - Uhifadhi wa ndani kukuweka umeingia kati ya vipindi - Data ya kipindi kudumisha kikapu chako - Kumbukumbu za kuvinjari ndani ya KenteXa (kurasa ulizofungua, utafutaji, bidhaa ulizotazama na vitufe vichache tulivyochagua) kuelewa jinsi jukwaa linavyotumika na kukuonyesha yanayokuhusu - Ripoti za hitilafu (ukurasa, hitilafu na aina ya kivinjari) ili kurekebisha matatizo Hatutumii wafuatiliaji wa matangazo wa wahusika wengine wala kuuza data ya kuvinjari kwa watangazaji.

6. Haki Zako

Una haki ya: - Kufikia data yako binafsi wakati wowote kupitia ukurasa wa Wasifu - Kusasisha au kurekebisha taarifa zako - Kuomba kufutwa kwa akaunti na data yako - Kuzima habari na ofa za KenteXa wakati wowote: Wasifu → Mipangilio → Taarifa za KenteXa (taarifa za maagizo, malipo na mizigo yako zinaendelea) Kutumia haki hizi, wasiliana nasi kupitia support@kentexa.com

7. Uhifadhi wa Data

Tunahifadhi data yako kwa muda akaunti yako ikiwa hai. Baada ya kufutwa kwa akaunti: - Kumbukumbu za miamala zinahifadhiwa kwa miaka 7 (sharti la kisheria) - Data ya wasifu binafsi inafutwa ndani ya siku 30 - Kumbukumbu za kuvinjari (kurasa, utafutaji, mibofyo) na mada ulizofuata zinafutwa mara moja - Kumbukumbu za ufuatiliaji wa vifurushi zinahifadhiwa kwa miaka 2 Kumbukumbu za kuvinjari zinafutwa zenyewe baada ya miezi 12 hata akaunti ikiwa hai.

8. Faragha ya Watoto

KenteXa haikusudiwi kwa watumiaji chini ya miaka 18. Hatukusanyi kwa makusudi taarifa binafsi kutoka kwa watoto wadogo. Tukigundua mtoto mdogo ameunda akaunti, tutaifuta mara moja.

9. Mabadiliko ya Sera Hii

Tunaweza kusasisha Sera hii ya Faragha mara kwa mara. Tutakuarifu kuhusu mabadiliko makubwa kupitia barua pepe au arifa ya jukwaa. Kuendelea kutumia KenteXa baada ya mabadiliko kunamaanisha umekubali.

10. Wasiliana Nasi

Kwa maswali yanayohusiana na faragha au kutumia haki zako za data: Barua pepe: support@kentexa.com Tovuti: kentexa.com Kampuni: Bishoo Intelligence Systems (BiS), Tanzania

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · We respect your privacy

This policy covers the kentexa.com website and the KenteXa app on Google Play (com.app.bishoo, formerly Bishoo Tanzania), operated by Bishoo Intelligence Systems (BiS), Tanzania.

Your privacy matters to us. KenteXa does not sell your personal data. We collect only what is necessary to operate the marketplace safely.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use KenteXa: Personal Information: - Full name, email address, phone number - Delivery address and location - National ID number (for agents and offices only) Transaction Information: - Orders placed, products purchased, payment amounts - Invoice numbers and transaction references - Parcel tracking history Technical Information: - Device type, browser, IP address - Pages visited, time spent on platform - Search queries and browsing history on KenteXa Inbox Messages: - Messages you send and receive in the KenteXa inbox (including WhatsApp and Instagram connected by a business), photos and a business's internal notes

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: - Process orders and enable transactions between buyers and sellers - Send OTP verification codes and account notifications - Generate invoices, receipts and tracking numbers - Show you posts, businesses, products and services relevant to you - Prevent fraud and ensure platform security - Communicate important platform updates - Improve our services through analytics Your interests: We use your activity on KenteXa (what you search, view, like or save, the topics, communities and businesses you follow, tags and your orders) to understand what interests you and show you relevant content. We also look at overall demand (for example things many people search for but do not find) to improve KenteXa; those reports cover groups of 10 people or more and never name you. Authorised staff may see one person's interest summary only for support or safety, and every view is recorded. Your inbox messages are not used for interests. Artificial intelligence (AI): We use AI services from outside providers (such as DeepSeek, OpenAI and Anthropic), which may be outside Tanzania, for tasks such as understanding your searches, suggesting product descriptions, checking comments before they are published (comments that break the rules may be refused) and analysing interests. We send only what the task needs, and we do not send your name or phone number for interest analysis. AI alone never makes decisions about your account, your payments or your verification. Inbox messages: Authorised KenteXa staff (admin, managers, customer care and content moderators) may read inbox conversations for safety, fraud prevention, resolving disputes and supporting you. Every conversation a staff member opens is recorded with their name. We do not use your messages for advertising and never sell them.

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only when necessary: With Sellers: Your delivery address, name and phone number are shared with sellers to fulfill your order. With Payment Processors: Your phone number is shared with M-Pesa, Airtel, Tigo or other payment providers to process payments. With Delivery Agents: Your delivery address, name and phone are shared with offices and local agents to deliver your parcel. With Authorities: We may share information with law enforcement if required by Tanzanian law. We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

We protect your data using: - Encrypted database storage - Secure HTTPS connections for all data transmission - JWT token-based authentication - Password hashing (bcrypt) — we never store plain passwords - Regular security audits No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users within 72 hours.

5. Cookies & Tracking

KenteXa uses: - Local storage to keep you logged in between sessions - Session data to maintain your cart - Browsing records within KenteXa (pages opened, searches, products viewed and a few buttons we chose) to understand how the platform is used and show you what is relevant - Error reports (page, error and browser type) to fix problems We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell browsing data to advertisers.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to: - Access your personal data at any time via your Profile page - Update or correct your information - Request deletion of your account and data - Turn off KenteXa news and offers at any time: Profile → Settings → KenteXa news and offers (messages about your orders, payments and parcels still arrive) To exercise these rights, contact us at support@kentexa.com

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. After account deletion: - Transaction records are kept for 7 years (legal requirement) - Personal profile data is deleted within 30 days - Browsing records (pages, searches, taps) and followed topics are deleted at once - Parcel tracking records are kept for 2 years Browsing records are deleted automatically after 12 months even while your account is active.

8. Children's Privacy

KenteXa is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we discover a minor has created an account, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or platform notification. Your continued use of KenteXa after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your data rights: Email: support@kentexa.com Website: kentexa.com Company: Bishoo Intelligence Systems (BiS), Tanzania
