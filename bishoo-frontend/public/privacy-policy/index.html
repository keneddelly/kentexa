Sera ya Faragha

Ilisasishwa mwisho: Novemba 2026 · Tunaheshimu faragha yako

Sera hii inahusu tovuti ya kentexa.com na programu ya KenteXa kwenye Google Play (com.app.bishoo, iliyoitwa zamani Bishoo Tanzania), inayoendeshwa na Bishoo Intelligence Systems (BiS), Tanzania.

Faragha yako ni muhimu kwetu. KenteXa haiuzi data yako binafsi. Tunakusanya tu kile kinachohitajika kuendesha soko kwa usalama.

1. Taarifa Tunazokusanya

Tunakusanya taarifa zifuatazo unapotumia KenteXa: Taarifa Binafsi: - Jina kamili, anwani ya barua pepe, namba ya simu - Anwani ya utoaji na mahali - Namba ya kitambulisho cha taifa (kwa mawakala na ofisi pekee) Taarifa za Miamala: - Maagizo yaliyowekwa, bidhaa zilizonunuliwa, kiasi cha malipo - Namba za ankara na marejeleo ya miamala - Historia ya ufuatiliaji wa vifurushi Taarifa za Kiufundi: - Aina ya kifaa, kivinjari, anwani ya IP - Kurasa ulizotembelea, muda uliotumika kwenye jukwaa - Maswali ya utafutaji na historia ya kuvinjari kwenye KenteXa Ujumbe wa Inbox: - Ujumbe unaotuma na kupokea kwenye inbox ya KenteXa (pamoja na WhatsApp na Instagram zilizounganishwa na biashara), picha na maelezo ya ndani ya biashara

2. Jinsi Tunavyotumia Taarifa Zako

Tunatumia taarifa zako kufanya: - Kushughulikia maagizo na kuwezesha miamala kati ya wanunuzi na wauzaji - Kutuma misimbo ya uthibitisho wa OTP na arifa za akaunti - Kutengeneza ankara, risiti na namba za kufuatilia - Kukuonyesha bidhaa na matangazo yanayokuhusu - Kuzuia udanganyifu na kuhakikisha usalama wa jukwaa - Kuwasiliana na masasisho muhimu ya jukwaa - Kuboresha huduma zetu kupitia uchambuzi wa data Ujumbe wa inbox: Wafanyakazi wa KenteXa walioruhusiwa (admin, meneja, huduma kwa wateja na wakaguzi wa maudhui) wanaweza kusoma mazungumzo ya inbox kwa ajili ya usalama, kuzuia udanganyifu, kutatua migogoro na kukusaidia. Kila mazungumzo yanayofunguliwa na mfanyakazi yanarekodiwa pamoja na jina lake. Hatutumii ujumbe wako kwa matangazo wala kuuuza.

3. Kushiriki Taarifa

Tunashiriki taarifa zako tu inapohitajika: Kwa Wauzaji: Anwani yako ya utoaji, jina na namba ya simu vinashirikiwa na wauzaji ili kutimiza agizo lako. Kwa Wasimamizi wa Malipo: Namba yako ya simu inashirikiwa na M-Pesa, Airtel, Tigo au watoa huduma wengine wa malipo ili kushughulikia malipo. Kwa Mawakala wa Utoaji: Anwani yako ya utoaji, jina na simu vinashirikiwa na ofisi na mawakala wa karibu ili kupeleka kifurushi chako. Kwa Mamlaka: Tunaweza kushiriki taarifa na vyombo vya sheria ikiwa sheria za Tanzania zinahitaji hivyo. HATUUZI data yako binafsi kwa wahusika wengine kwa madhumuni ya masoko.

4. Usalama wa Data

Tunalinda data yako kwa kutumia: - Uhifadhi wa hifadhidata uliosimbwa - Muunganisho salama wa HTTPS kwa uhamishaji wote wa data - Uthibitishaji wa JWT token - Usimbaji wa nywila (bcrypt) — hatuhifadhi nywila wazi kamwe - Ukaguzi wa mara kwa mara wa usalama Hakuna mfumo ulio salama 100%. Iwapo kutatokea uvunjaji wa data, tutawaarifu watumiaji walioathirika ndani ya masaa 72.

5. Vidakuzi na Ufuatiliaji

KenteXa inatumia: - Uhifadhi wa ndani kukuweka umeingia kati ya vipindi - Data ya kipindi kudumisha kikapu chako - Uchambuzi wa msingi kuelewa jinsi watumiaji wanavyotumia jukwaa Hatutumii wafuatiliaji wa matangazo wa wahusika wengine wala kuuza data ya kuvinjari kwa watangazaji.

6. Haki Zako

Una haki ya: - Kufikia data yako binafsi wakati wowote kupitia ukurasa wa Wasifu - Kusasisha au kurekebisha taarifa zako - Kuomba kufutwa kwa akaunti na data yako - Kuondoa idhini ya mawasiliano ya masoko Kutumia haki hizi, wasiliana nasi kupitia support@kentexa.com

7. Uhifadhi wa Data

Tunahifadhi data yako kwa muda akaunti yako ikiwa hai. Baada ya kufutwa kwa akaunti: - Kumbukumbu za miamala zinahifadhiwa kwa miaka 7 (sharti la kisheria) - Data ya wasifu binafsi inafutwa ndani ya siku 30 - Kumbukumbu za ufuatiliaji wa vifurushi zinahifadhiwa kwa miaka 2

8. Faragha ya Watoto

KenteXa haikusudiwi kwa watumiaji chini ya miaka 18. Hatukusanyi kwa makusudi taarifa binafsi kutoka kwa watoto wadogo. Tukigundua mtoto mdogo ameunda akaunti, tutaifuta mara moja.

9. Mabadiliko ya Sera Hii

Tunaweza kusasisha Sera hii ya Faragha mara kwa mara. Tutakuarifu kuhusu mabadiliko makubwa kupitia barua pepe au arifa ya jukwaa. Kuendelea kutumia KenteXa baada ya mabadiliko kunamaanisha umekubali.

10. Wasiliana Nasi

Kwa maswali yanayohusiana na faragha au kutumia haki zako za data: Barua pepe: support@kentexa.com Tovuti: kentexa.com Kampuni: Bishoo Intelligence Systems (BiS), Tanzania

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · We respect your privacy

This policy covers the kentexa.com website and the KenteXa app on Google Play (com.app.bishoo, formerly Bishoo Tanzania), operated by Bishoo Intelligence Systems (BiS), Tanzania.

Your privacy matters to us. KenteXa does not sell your personal data. We collect only what is necessary to operate the marketplace safely.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use KenteXa: Personal Information: - Full name, email address, phone number - Delivery address and location - National ID number (for agents and offices only) Transaction Information: - Orders placed, products purchased, payment amounts - Invoice numbers and transaction references - Parcel tracking history Technical Information: - Device type, browser, IP address - Pages visited, time spent on platform - Search queries and browsing history on KenteXa Inbox Messages: - Messages you send and receive in the KenteXa inbox (including WhatsApp and Instagram connected by a business), photos and a business's internal notes

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: - Process orders and facilitate transactions between buyers and sellers - Send OTP verification codes and account notifications - Generate invoices, receipts and tracking numbers - Show you relevant products and listings - Prevent fraud and ensure platform security - Communicate important platform updates - Improve our services through analytics Inbox messages: Authorised KenteXa staff (admin, managers, customer care and content moderators) may read inbox conversations for safety, fraud prevention, resolving disputes and supporting you. Every conversation a staff member opens is recorded with their name. We do not use your messages for advertising and never sell them.

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only when necessary: With Sellers: Your delivery address, name and phone number are shared with sellers to fulfill your order. With Payment Processors: Your phone number is shared with M-Pesa, Airtel, Tigo or other payment providers to process payments. With Delivery Agents: Your delivery address, name and phone are shared with offices and local agents to deliver your parcel. With Authorities: We may share information with law enforcement if required by Tanzanian law. We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

We protect your data using: - Encrypted database storage - Secure HTTPS connections for all data transmission - JWT token-based authentication - Password hashing (bcrypt) — we never store plain passwords - Regular security audits No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users within 72 hours.

5. Cookies & Tracking

KenteXa uses: - Local storage to keep you logged in between sessions - Session data to maintain your cart - Basic analytics to understand how users navigate the platform We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell browsing data to advertisers.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to: - Access your personal data at any time via your Profile page - Update or correct your information - Request deletion of your account and data - Withdraw consent for marketing communications To exercise these rights, contact us at support@kentexa.com

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. After account deletion: - Transaction records are kept for 7 years (legal requirement) - Personal profile data is deleted within 30 days - Parcel tracking records are kept for 2 years

8. Children's Privacy

KenteXa is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we discover a minor has created an account, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or platform notification. Your continued use of KenteXa after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your data rights: Email: support@kentexa.com Website: kentexa.com Company: Bishoo Intelligence Systems (BiS), Tanzania
