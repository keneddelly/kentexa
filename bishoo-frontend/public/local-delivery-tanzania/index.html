Local delivery · Commerce and logistics

Move local orders from business to customer with clearer handovers.

Local delivery is more than the final trip to a customer's door. It starts with an order, a prepared parcel, a collection or office handover, and a clear path to the recipient. Kentexa connects commerce with logistics workflows so these steps can work together.

From an order to a shipment

A business prepares goods for dispatch and creates a shipment with the relevant parcel and delivery details. The shipment workflow provides a structured starting point for moving an order beyond the seller's location.

Coordinate collection and handovers

Depending on the selected arrangement, a parcel may be collected by an Agent, handed to a Super Agent at a hub or office, or transferred to a Transport Provider for movement between locations. Each handover is part of the shipment journey.

Keep delivery expectations clear

Delivery availability, price, timing and coverage depend on the route and participating operators. Businesses should confirm the service and charges available for the specific shipment before dispatch.

A network that can grow by location

Kentexa's logistics model is designed to connect businesses, hubs, agents and transport capacity. Coverage should be described by the participating network—not assumed to be nationwide.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kentexa deliver every order itself?

Kentexa coordinates platform workflows and participating logistics roles; the actual movement is handled according to the selected provider and service arrangement.

Is local delivery available throughout Tanzania?

No nationwide coverage should be assumed. Availability depends on the businesses, agents, hubs and transport providers participating in a particular area.

Can a seller choose a delivery arrangement?

The shipment flow supports selecting available logistics arrangements. Options depend on the shipment, route and enabled services.
