Frequently asked questions

What businesses can I find?

Depending on published profiles and listings, discovery may include shops, service providers, hospitality businesses and other local businesses.

Are all businesses in Tanzania listed?

No. Results depend on businesses that have joined and published information on Kentexa.

How can my business be discovered?

Create or claim the appropriate business presence and keep its profile, location, contact details and offerings accurate. Available onboarding steps are shown on the platform.
