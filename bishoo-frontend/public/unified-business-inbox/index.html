Frequently asked questions

Does the inbox currently connect every social channel?

No. Do not assume every external channel is available. Each integration depends on its implementation, provider approval and successful account authorization.

Can a business use it for sales and support?

That is the intended workflow: keeping customer enquiries close to business activity. Available tools depend on the current account and enabled features.

Why does a channel need authorization?

External platforms require businesses to grant permissions through their official connection process. The connection must complete successfully before messages can be handled.
