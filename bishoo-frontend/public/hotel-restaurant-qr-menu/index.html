Let guests open your menu with a single scan.

A QR menu gives restaurant, café and hotel guests a convenient way to view what a venue offers using their own phone. It can make menu access simpler while giving the business a digital way to present its items.

Give guests quick access to the menu

A QR code placed on a table, counter or entrance gives guests a direct path to the venue's digital menu. They can browse from their own phone without searching for a website or waiting for a printed menu.

Keep menu information clear

Organise items into understandable categories and provide readable descriptions and prices. Hospitality businesses should review the published menu regularly so guests are not relying on outdated information.

Make the venue easier to discover

A digital menu can complement a hotel's or restaurant's wider online business presence. It helps customers understand what the venue offers before or during a visit.

Know what the QR menu does—and does not do

A menu page is not automatically a reservation, ordering, point-of-sale or payment system. Those capabilities should only be advertised when they are separately enabled for the venue.

Frequently asked questions

How does a QR menu work?

A guest scans the venue's QR code with a compatible phone camera and opens the linked digital menu in a browser.

Can a restaurant update its menu?

Menu editing and publishing options depend on the business account and the features enabled for that venue. Keep prices and availability current wherever the menu is managed.

Does a QR menu replace the restaurant's ordering or payment system?

Not necessarily. A QR menu can present information; ordering, table service and payment are separate workflows unless explicitly enabled and connected.
