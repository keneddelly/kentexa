About Kentexa · Tanzania

A connected platform for the way people discover, do business and move goods.

Kentexa is a Tanzania-focused digital platform developed to connect people, sellers, businesses and service providers. It brings online marketplace discovery together with business operations, social communities, customer communication and logistics workflows in one connected experience.

Explore Kentexa

What makes Kentexa different?

Kentexa is designed as more than a product-listing website. Its direction is to connect the steps around everyday commerce: discovering an item or service, finding the business behind it, communicating with the provider, managing business activity and arranging the movement of goods. Each capability has its own workflow and records, while the wider platform helps those capabilities work together.

AI-assisted discovery can help people express what they need in natural language, but product availability, prices, orders, payments and shipment status remain grounded in their authoritative platform and business records.

What people and businesses can use

Marketplace and online selling

Product listings, seller storefronts and marketplace discovery help customers find goods and connect with sellers.

POS and stock management

Business tools support everyday sales and inventory workflows, from recording transactions to keeping stock information organised.

Social communities

Topic-based spaces give people and businesses a place to exchange knowledge, discuss interests and discover useful local opportunities.

Unified business inbox

A shared communication workspace is designed to bring customer conversations closer to sales and support. External channels depend on successful provider authorization and enabled integrations.

Logistics and delivery

Shipment, parcel, logistics office, hub, agent and transport-provider workflows support coordinated movement and handovers.

Local business and service discovery

Business profiles and published listings help people explore shops, service providers and other businesses.

Hospitality tools

QR menu experiences help hotels and restaurants present menus and connect customers to the services enabled by each business.

AI-assisted discovery

Intent-led search is designed to help people describe a need and find relevant products, services or businesses from available platform information.

Who Kentexa is for

Customers can discover products, businesses and services. Sellers and retailers can present products and manage commerce activity. Businesses and service providers can build a presence and manage customer-facing workflows. Logistics offices, agents and transport providers can participate in shipment and parcel handovers where those workflows are enabled.

Built with Tanzania in mind

Kentexa is focused on the Tanzanian market and the practical needs of local commerce. The platform is designed around mobile-first access and the people and businesses involved in buying, selling, providing services and moving goods. Listings, integrations, routes and individual capabilities depend on active participation, account configuration and current service coverage; not every feature or service is available in every location.

Developed by Bishoo Intelligence Systems

Kentexa is developed by Bishoo Intelligence Systems (BIS). BIS is the technology company behind the platform; Kentexa is the digital product through which people and businesses access its connected commerce, business and logistics capabilities.
