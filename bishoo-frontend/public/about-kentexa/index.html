About Kentexa · Tanzania

One connected platform for commerce, businesses, communities and logistics.

Kentexa is a Tanzania-focused digital platform being built to connect people with products, businesses, services and the networks that help commerce happen. It brings marketplace discovery together with business tools, social interaction, customer communication and logistics workflows.

Explore Kentexa

Why Kentexa exists

People often need to move between separate places to discover a business, ask a question, find a product, arrange delivery or manage daily business activity. Kentexa's direction is to make these connected activities easier to reach through one platform, while keeping the underlying business, payment and logistics records authoritative.

What you can explore

Marketplace and discovery

Explore product listings, sellers, businesses and services through a connected local commerce experience.

Business tools and POS

Tools for businesses to manage sales and stock activity, with a focus on practical day-to-day operations.

Social communities

Community spaces connect people through shared interests, discussions and local opportunities.

Unified business inbox

A shared communication workspace designed to bring customer conversations closer to business workflows. Channel availability depends on integrations enabled.

Logistics and delivery

Shipment, parcel, hub, agent and transport workflows designed to connect the movement of goods.

Local business and service discovery

Help people find relevant shops, service providers and other local businesses based on available listings.

Built for the Tanzanian market

Kentexa is focused on the needs of people and businesses in Tanzania. The platform is designed with local commerce and logistics realities in mind, including mobile-first access and the roles played by sellers, service providers, agents, hubs and transport partners. Availability of individual features and services can vary as the platform evolves.

Developed by Bishoo Intelligence Systems

Kentexa is developed by Bishoo Intelligence Systems (BIS), a technology company. Kentexa is the connected platform; BIS is the company behind its development.
