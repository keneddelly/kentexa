KenteXa is a Tanzanian platform for conversation, commerce and logistics. Writers publish articles (Makala); readers can read them without signing in, continue the discussion in communities and share them on WhatsApp, Facebook and elsewhere.

Who it is for

Writers and journalists

Analysis, long-form articles and commentary on current events.

Professionals

Accountants, lawyers, engineers, doctors, teachers: share what you know in plain language.

Entrepreneurs and business owners

Lessons learned, business methods and real challenges.

Academics and researchers

Explain your research to everyday readers.

Technology and finance analysts

Digital payments, AI, personal finance and the economy.

Community leaders

Education, health, agriculture and local development.

What you get

Your writer profile

Name, photo, bio, the topics you cover and all your articles in one place: kentexa.com/@name.

The article editor

Title, cover photo, sub-headings, lists, quotes and photos inside the text, up to 20,000 characters.

Real readers

Reader count on every article, comments, likes and shares.

Discovery

Topics, hashtags, topic pages and the "New articles" row on the home page.

Series

Group your articles into Part 1, 2, 3; readers follow and are notified of new parts.

Writer of the Week

Every week KenteXa recognises the writer who started the best conversation.

How to start

  1. Press Start writing and open an account with Google or email.
  2. Add a photo and a short bio about you and what you know.
  3. Write your first article and choose its topic.
  4. Share it with your readers and reply to comments.

Plainly

No false promises. KenteXa does not promise payment or a number of readers. Work must be your own, lawful and respectful, following the writing guidelines. Opinions in articles are the writer's own.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to write on KenteXa?

Nothing. There is no joining or publishing fee.

Will I be paid for writing?

Not at present. KenteXa does not promise payment or a number of readers. What you gain is your name, readers and conversations; earning options may come later and will be announced clearly.

What can I write about?

Business, technology, finance, agriculture, transport, jobs, education, policy, entrepreneurship and other topics on Tanzania and East Africa, as long as the work is yours and follows the writing guidelines.

Who owns my article?

You do. By publishing you allow KenteXa to show and distribute it on the platform; you can delete it at any time.

Do I need a phone number?

No. You can join with Google or email. A phone is asked only for features that need one, such as selling or sending parcels.
