Search with natural language

A person can describe a need in everyday language rather than guessing a catalogue keyword. For example: 'I need a reliable phone repair shop near my area' expresses both a service need and a location context.

Understand intent before choosing a domain

A request may be about buying a product, finding a service, discovering a business or arranging logistics. Intent helps route the request to the appropriate Kentexa capability; it does not replace the authoritative product, order, price or shipment records.

Relevant results depend on real supply

Search quality depends on accurate listings, business information, location data and available services. AI cannot make an unavailable product or provider available, or invent prices and operating details.

Frequently asked questions

Does AI search guarantee an exact answer?

No. It can help interpret a request and surface relevant options, but results depend on available and accurate platform data.

Can it search for services as well as products?

Kentexa's discovery direction includes products, services and local businesses. The available result types depend on enabled features and published listings.

Does AI decide prices or shipment status?

No. Prices, inventory, orders and shipment status must come from their authoritative business or platform systems.
