Communities organised around topics

People can gather around interests, skills, local topics and practical needs. Topic-focused spaces help conversations stay relevant and make it easier for new members to understand what a community is about.

Useful conversations create discovery

A member may ask for a recommendation, share an experience or discuss a local opportunity. These conversations can help people discover businesses, products and services—without turning every community discussion into an advertisement.

A place for people and businesses

Communities can help individuals exchange knowledge and help businesses participate in relevant conversations. Participation should add value to the topic and respect community expectations.

Frequently asked questions

What are Kentexa communities for?

They provide social spaces for people to connect and participate in conversations around shared interests and topics.

Are communities only for businesses?

No. The concept supports people and businesses participating around relevant interests, knowledge and local topics.

Can a community guarantee customers for a business?

No. Communities support discovery and conversation; they do not guarantee reach, sales or customer outcomes.
