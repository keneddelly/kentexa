Kentexa Super Agents operate local hubs that support parcel movement between sellers, customers, delivery agents and transport providers. The role is designed for people or businesses with a suitable operating location and the ability to coordinate reliable parcel handoffs.

What does a Kentexa Super Agent do?

Receive and organise parcels

Receive parcels at your hub, record handoffs and keep shipments moving through the correct stages.

Coordinate local delivery agents

Work with agents who handle collection and last-mile delivery in your service area.

Connect cities and transport

Coordinate dispatches and handoffs with transport providers and destination hubs.

Manage hub operations

Use Kentexa's logistics tools to monitor parcel activity and manage supported hub operations.

What you get

Senders find you

People sending a parcel on KenteXa see nearby offices, their prices and phone numbers, and choose yours.

Sellers ship through you

Online sellers pick your office to send their orders to other regions.

Your own prices

Set your price per trip or per kilo; the customer sees it before bringing the parcel.

Receipts and SMS

Every parcel gets a tracking number; sender and receiver get SMS, and you print a receipt at the counter.

Who should apply?

This opportunity may suit an established courier office, a logistics business, a shop or business with a suitable secure location, or an entrepreneur able to operate a dependable parcel hub.

  • A real, reachable operating address in the city you want to serve.
  • A reliable phone number and the ability to communicate with customers and delivery partners.
  • Capacity to receive, safeguard, organise and hand over parcels responsibly.
  • Willingness to follow Kentexa's parcel verification, dispatch and handoff procedures.
  • Government identification and any supporting information requested during the application process.

How the application works

  1. Read the role information and open the Super Agent application form.
  2. Sign in or create a KenteXa account when prompted (Google sign-in works).
  3. Provide your business name, city, address, phone number and identification details.
  4. Submit your application for review by Kentexa.
  5. Once approved and activated, access your Super Agent Hub and follow the operating guidance.

Important: Submitting an application does not automatically activate a hub or guarantee earnings. Applications are subject to review, approval and the applicable Kentexa operating terms and rates.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Kentexa Super Agent?

A Super Agent operates a local parcel hub and coordinates parcel receiving, sorting, dispatch and handoffs with delivery agents and transport providers.

How much does it cost?

Joining is free and the first 20 parcels are free. After that it is TZS 500 per parcel you charged for; if you charged less, you pay only what you charged. One invoice a month, 30 days to pay.

What if I do not pay the fee on time?

Your work continues. If the debt reaches TZS 50,000 and part of it is more than 30 days unpaid, new work pauses until you pay. You get an SMS warning 3 days before.

Can I apply without a Kentexa account?

Yes. Start the application and create a Kentexa account when prompted. You can then submit your application.

Does submitting an application activate my hub?

No. Kentexa reviews applications. Hub access is subject to approval and activation.
