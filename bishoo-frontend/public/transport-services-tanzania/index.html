Connect transport capacity to real shipment journeys.

Transport is one stage in a larger logistics journey. A parcel must be received, assigned, moved between locations and handed over correctly. Kentexa's logistics model connects transport providers with businesses and logistics offices around those shipment stages.

Transport providers in the shipment chain

A Transport Provider supplies movement capacity for parcels between locations. The shipment remains the central record, while transport assignments describe who is responsible for a movement stage.

Connect offices and hubs

A Super Agent operates a hub or logistics office where parcels can be received, organised and handed over. This creates a coordination point between local collection and longer-distance transport.

Support the final handover

After a parcel reaches its destination office or hub, an Agent may support the last-mile handover to the recipient. The roles remain distinct so responsibility at each stage can be understood.

Route schedules and capacity

Scheduled movement can help consolidate parcels and make transport capacity easier to plan. Specific routes, departure times, prices and availability must be confirmed from active providers; planned corridors are not proof of live service.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kentexa own or operate every vehicle?

The platform's model connects participating transport providers; it does not mean Kentexa itself operates every vehicle or route.

Can providers set their own route prices?

Transport arrangements and provider pricing depend on the configured provider workflow and agreed service terms. Confirm the applicable price before assigning a shipment.

Are all routes in Tanzania available?

No. Route availability depends on active providers and enabled locations. Check the current platform options for the shipment.
