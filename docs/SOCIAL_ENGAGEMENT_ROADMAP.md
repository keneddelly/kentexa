## 1. Product objective

Build a social experience that gives people meaningful reasons to publish, respond, participate, return, discover identities and conduct commerce. Success is not measured by likes alone.

Kentexa's product philosophy in `CLAUDE.md` remains authoritative:

`Identity → Content → Interaction → Trust → Commerce`

Kentexa is a digital business identity and commerce network, not merely a product-listing marketplace. Personal users, businesses, service providers, professionals, agents and organizations must all be able to participate according to their capabilities.

## 3. Existing product and design decisions

### Agreed direction

1. **Both social and commerce engagement.** Posts should support natural social participation while allowing relevant business outcomes. Do not make every post an advertisement.
2. **Preserve the established Home Feed identity.** The preferred visual language is the existing clean Twitter-style feed: professional, bold readable icons, no decorative emojis, mobile-first, clear touch targets and readable text.
3. **Compact Home Feed header.** Keep the Kentexa logo and key navigation actions compact.
4. **Inline AI search.** Keep the AI search entry visible without allowing it to dominate the screen.
5. **Keep Moments.** Moments/Stories remain part of the feed and identity experience; do not remove or replace them.
6. **Media should support the content.** Do not force all photos into one crop or make every post a full-screen image. Preserve the full media when opened.
7. **No coding during product-design approval.** Mockups and specifications are reviewed first.

### Design hypotheses — not yet final approvals

- A hybrid visual approach is preferred: Instagram-quality media presentation with Twitter-like feed density, conversation and navigation.
- Candidate image previews: square (1:1) for many catalogue/product posts; portrait (4:5) for suitable lifestyle/editorial posts; landscape (16:9) for wide scenes and announcements. These are starting points to validate, not hard constraints. The original image should remain accessible.
- A compact post composer may let users select a purpose/type, but the exact creation flow is still to be decided.
- AI may suggest optional ways to invite meaningful engagement. It must never rewrite or publish without the author's explicit action.
- Feed ranking should ultimately balance relevance, relationships, freshness and meaningful engagement rather than raw popularity alone. Exact ranking rules remain open.

## 5. Candidate post formats

These are proposed formats to evaluate against existing functionality. Do not create duplicate post models before auditing the current domain.

| Candidate format | Purpose | Possible actions |
|---|---|---|
| General/social post | Share thoughts, photos, videos or updates | Like, comment, repost, share, save |
| Question/discussion | Invite opinions, advice and conversation | Reply, follow discussion, share |
| Poll | Collect structured opinions | Vote, view results, discuss |
| Showcase | Present an identity, product, service or work | Like, comment, repost, enquire, view details, save |
| Offer | Communicate a promotion or special offer | View offer, enquire, claim if supported, share |
| Looking for | Express demand and request recommendations | Recommend, respond, contact |
| Business update | Keep an identity active and informed | Follow, comment, enquire, message |

A post's **content type**, **engagement mechanics**, and **business outcome/action** are separate concepts. For example, a showcase can allow comments and saves while also linking to a product. A poll is a participation mechanic and must not be treated as a product listing.

All features must respect identity, role and capability rules in `CLAUDE.md`. Do not assume every user is a seller or every business has inventory, shipping or checkout capabilities.

## 6. Engagement principles

- Optimize for meaningful participation, not empty activity or vanity metrics.
- Keep actions context-sensitive. Do not show irrelevant commerce actions on a personal discussion.
- Make commenting and replying easy and coherent.
- Distinguish in-platform reposting from external sharing.
- Avoid forced polls, forced questions, artificial prompts and engagement bait.
- Never fabricate counts, votes, views, reviews, orders or enquiries.
- Protect users from spam, harassment, manipulation and unwanted contact.
- Provide reporting, blocking, hiding and moderation paths.
- Preserve the author's control over content and publication.
- AI suggestions are optional, transparent and user-approved.

## 7. Measurement framework

Evaluate the full journey, not just likes:

- Reach and qualified views (with clear, deduplicated definitions).
- View-to-meaningful-interaction rate.
- Comment and reply participation / discussion depth.
- Poll participation and completion.
- Reposts and external shares.
- Saves and subsequent returns.
- Profile visits and follows attributable to content.
- Product/service detail views attributable to content.
- Enquiries, conversations, bookings and purchases attributable to content where reliable attribution exists.
- Retention: users who return to participate, not only scroll.

Define event names, deduplication, attribution windows, privacy, authorization and data retention only after auditing the existing event/analytics infrastructure. Do not introduce a second competing event system. Follow the centralized activity/event principles in `CLAUDE.md`.

## 9. Design deliverables required before implementation

Prepare and review these in order:

1. **Experience map:** user journeys for casual user, creator, business, service provider and viewer/customer.
2. **Information architecture:** where create, feed, Moments, discussions, profiles, notifications and insights live.
3. **Screen inventory:** every screen, modal, drawer, sheet, state and navigation transition.
4. **Visual system:** preserve Kentexa's approved typography, color, icon and spacing language; define media treatment and responsive behavior.
5. **High-fidelity mockups:** Home Feed, composer, each post format, post detail, comments, poll voting/results, offer/showcase actions and analytics.
6. **Interaction specifications:** tap behavior, loading, success, failure, empty, disabled, logged-out, edit/delete and moderation states.
7. **Business rules:** who can publish each format, which actions are allowed, identity attribution, privacy, visibility and lifecycle.
8. **Technical specification:** reuse map, data model proposal, API contracts, event model, permissions, migrations, backwards compatibility and performance.
9. **Acceptance criteria:** observable behavior for each screen and API flow.
10. **Implementation plan:** small reviewable phases, dependency order, tests and rollback strategy.

The product owner must approve the designs and technical scope before Claude begins implementation.

## 10. Suggested delivery phases (not implementation authorization)

- **Discovery:** read-only audit and current-state map.
- **Design foundation:** user journeys, feed and composer experience.
- **Core interaction:** unify and refine existing reactions, comments, replies, reposts/shares, saves and follow paths where needed.
- **Participation formats:** question/discussion and polls, only after domain and UX approval.
- **Commerce-connected content:** showcase, offer, recommendation and enquiry flows, reusing authoritative product/service/business records.
- **Intelligence and analytics:** meaningful performance insights and optional AI suggestions after reliable event definitions exist.
- **Optimization:** accessibility, mobile performance, feed relevance, abuse prevention and measured iteration.

Phase boundaries and order may change after the audit. Do not create speculative migrations or build all formats at once.
