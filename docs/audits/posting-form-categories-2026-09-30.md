## Dedicated service form

All 12 service categories (ufundi, usafi, elimu, upishi, usafirishaji, afya, ubunifu, matengenezo, biashara, kilimo, nyumbani, mengineyo) share a four-step flow: category → title/photos/description → pricing → coverage. None asks product condition. Specialty, WhatsApp contact and working schedule are optional and collapsed. No working days or hours are invented. Negotiated/free-quote modes clear previously entered numeric prices at submission. Coverage area remains optional; primary coverage city and one image remain required.

## Existing requirements retained

Photo minimums, title/description, product stock, prices and existing brand/make/model requirements remain enforced. Digital products retain file and copyright requirements. This change simplifies questions without changing checkout, commissions, or inventory contracts.

## Further findings

Jobs still use the generic mandatory price rather than an optional salary. Free Stuff still requires a positive price. Correcting these requires an explicit listing pricing contract and consistent detail/search rendering; they are recorded here, not silently relaxed in a DTO. Services still use the existing coverage picker; coordinate-to-ward intelligence is a separate logistics requirement.
